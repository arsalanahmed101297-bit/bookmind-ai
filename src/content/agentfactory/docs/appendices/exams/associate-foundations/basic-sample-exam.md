**In everyday life.** You do not run a marathon before you have run a shorter race. A smaller test first shows you what you already know, and what to read again.

This is Panaversity's sample of the Basic Concepts Exam, Exam 1 on the way to [PCAO-F](overview.md#the-pcao-f-exams). It tests only [Claude and ChatGPT 2.0](../../../claude-and-chatgpt-2/overview.md), [The Five Horizons](../../../five-horizons.md), the [Preface](../../../preface.md) and [Part 0](../../../how-ai-works/overview.md). Everything else is like the full exam: work scenarios, one answer for each question, and the six domains of the CCAO-F exam guide, version 1.1.[^anthropic-ccao-f-guide] It has 35 questions and a 60-minute clock, so plan for about 1 minute 43 seconds a question. All 35 count.

The real Basic Concepts Exam has different questions. This sample is for practice: aim for 26 of 35 (74%). That is a study guide, not a pass mark.

When you finish, you see your score and a score for each domain. For every question you missed, you also see the page of this book that teaches it. Sign in first. Every attempt you finish is saved to your Panaversity account, so you can come back to it on any device.

[Start the timed exam](basic-sample-exam.sim.html "embed")

When you are ready for the whole syllabus, sit the [PCAO-F sample exam](sample-exam.md).
