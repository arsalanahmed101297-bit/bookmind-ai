**In everyday life.** A practice test with a clock tells you more than one more read through your notes. You find out what you know when time is short.

This is Panaversity's PCAO-F sample exam. It is practice for both PCAO-F and CCAO-F, because it follows the CCAO-F exam guide, version 1.1: its six domains, its weights and its 21 skills. It has 70 questions and a 120-minute clock, the same as the [PCAO-F exam](overview.md#the-pcao-f-exams) and the CCAO-F exam, so plan for about 1 minute 43 seconds a question. The CCAO-F exam leaves 10 of its 70 questions unscored; here all 70 count.[^anthropic-ccao-f-guide]

When you finish, you see your score and a score for each domain. For every question you missed, you also see the chapter and section of this book that teach it. Sign in first. Every attempt you finish is saved to your Panaversity account, so you can come back to it on any device.

[Start the timed exam](sample-exam.sim.html "embed")

To revise a domain that scored low, use the map in [Appendix C](overview.md).
