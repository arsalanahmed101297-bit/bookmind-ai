**In everyday life.** In your own town, you can buy what you forgot on the way. On a week's trip into the mountains, you cannot. Whatever you leave off the packing list stays at home. So you write the list before you go. When you come back, you add what you missed, ready for the next trip.

**What you will learn.** How the four Ds work as one loop, even when nobody is watching. The four beginner mistakes, the daily checks, and where each D goes deeper.

**Why it matters.** When nobody watches the work, a page written before it starts carries everything you would have said.

## The 4D loop

### How do the four Ds work as one loop?

This book joins the four Ds into one loop, the **4D loop**. You delegate, describe and discern, then go round again as needed, and you are diligent the whole way round.[^v1-ai-fluency]

### What does the loop look like when nobody is watching?

It spreads over days, and a person acts only at its two ends. Here is Brightline's monthly vendor-statement reconciliation, done that way. On Monday, August 24, at 9:00 a.m., three weeks after your chat, Dave writes its **4D plan**. It is a page with each D's lines and when each is done. Your 2:05 line, "change nothing", goes into it. He writes it in markdown, with the marks 0.2 teaches. The Preface asked you to write the rules you would give a worker for a weekly task. Those were Delegation and Description lines, and Dave's page adds the other two.

| The D | Dave's lines | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Delegation | Compare the statement with the register, and explain every difference. Change nothing, and contact nobody. Every correction waits for Dave. | Before the first run |
| Description | One row per difference, with its amount and its reason. Where the statement shows no reason, write "the statement does not show" instead of a guess. | Before the first run |
| Discernment | The next morning at 9:00, Dave reads the table and the run's record, and checks two differences against their invoices. | After each run |
| Diligence | Only the statement and the register go in. The table says that AI made it. Nothing reaches Buckeye Office Supply until Dave signs. | Throughout |

Now the same job, with nobody at the chat. On Tuesday, September 1, at 2:00 p.m., the run starts on its own, because it was set to start once the month ends. Nobody is watching it. It reads the page, the statement and the register, and it leaves a table and a record of what it did. On Wednesday at 9:00 a.m., Dave does what his Discernment line says. Both differences match their invoices, so he signs, ahead of the deadline on Thursday, September 3. If a check fails, Dave fixes the page before the next run, and the loop goes round again.

## A mistake in a chat, and in a run

### What are the four beginner mistakes?

Each one is a missing D.[^v1-ai-fluency] In a chat, you catch it at the next turn. In a run, nobody is there to catch it.

| The mistake | The missing D | In a run, it reaches the output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Typing before you decide the job | Delegation | No line says "change nothing", so the differences are removed. |
| Taking a weak first answer as the last | Description | The page is all the run gets, so what the page leaves out, the table leaves out. |
| Trusting a tidy answer because it looks finished | Discernment | Unless someone reads it at a set time, it goes into the accounts unread. |
| Thinking about data and responsibility too late | Diligence | Whatever the plan lets in goes in. With no line on who signs, nobody takes responsibility for the table. |

## A daily checklist

### What do you check every day, and when?

Four questions, one for each D.[^v1-ai-fluency] In a chat, you answer them as you go. When the AI works alone, the 4D plan answers them before the first run, except two: Discernment's question is decided before a run and answered only after it, and Diligence's is decided before and signed after.

| The D | Ask |
| --- | --- |
| Delegation | What is the goal? What does the AI do alone, and what waits for a person? |
| Description | What output, steps and behavior does the AI need? |
| Discernment | How will I know the output is right, and how it was made? |
| Diligence | What may go in? Who must know that AI was involved? Who signs? |

Before the AI works alone, Delegation also needs a setup check: the right account, access to what the job needs and no more, where the result will go, and which actions will ask you first.
