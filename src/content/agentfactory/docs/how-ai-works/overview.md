## What this part is about

Why can AI now take on whole tasks, like a worker? Part 0 answers that by showing what happens inside the apps while they work. It is written for everyone, and you need no technical background. It has four chapters, numbered 0.1 to 0.4.

Chapters 0.1 and 0.2 explain the machine and the language it reads and writes. Chapters 0.3 and 0.4 cover the human side, the skills and the judgment that working with it takes.

## Who can skip it

Only readers on the leader route who can already explain why AI sometimes sounds right and is wrong, and what to do about it. They start with [Part I](../ai-worker-paradigm/overview.md). Everyone else starts with [Chapter 0.1](what-ai-actually-is/overview.md).

## The four chapters

Each chapter has a hands-on lab and one piece of work for you to keep. The labs for all four chapters use Brightline Wholesale Supply, the company you met in [One job, start to finish](../claude-and-chatgpt-2/new-way-of-working/one-job.md). Its work of recording, checking and paying suppliers' invoices is called accounts payable (AP).

| Chapter | What it teaches | What you make |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0.1 [What AI Actually Is](what-ai-actually-is/overview.md) | What happens behind the AI text box. For example, AI predicts text instead of looking facts up, and nothing in it checks the truth. | AI field notes: a table of what each test showed, and where each answer came from |
| 0.2 [Markdown: The Language AI Reads and Writes](markdown/overview.md) | The AI writes markdown's marks, and the app turns them into headings and lists. You learn why the shape helps AI, and why a neat page is only as right as its lines. | A markdown page of Brightline's AP notes, each rule checked against its policy line |
| 0.3 [AI Fluency: The 4Ds](ai-fluency/overview.md) | Four skills for working with AI well: Delegation, Description, Discernment and Diligence, the 4Ds. They come from the AI Fluency Framework, by Rick Dakan and Joseph Feller with Anthropic. | A 4D plan for one Brightline AP task |
| 0.4 [Critical Thinking and Judgment in the AI Era](critical-thinking/overview.md) | Why judgment became the core skill once AI writes the first draft, and the checkpoint habit that keeps it. | A Brightline decision note: the recommendation, its evidence and assumptions, what would change it, and who decides |

## How Part 0 connects to Parts I and II

Part 0 gives you the ideas that Parts I and II build on.

- Chapter 0.1's last group of ideas shows how AI can use tools and work in steps. [Chapter 1](../ai-worker-paradigm/from-chatbots-to-ai-workers/overview.md), which opens Part I, starts from that idea.
- Chapter 0.3 ends with a map. For each of the 4Ds, it names the chapters in Part II that teach it in more depth.
- Anthropic's CCAO-F exam guide says the four Ds of [Chapter 0.3](ai-fluency/overview.md) underpin the objectives in every domain of the exam.[^anthropic-ccao-f-guide]
