## Friday morning

On Friday, October 8, Nisha came in early. Northgate's owner would call at 6 p.m. Before that, she wanted to understand her own week. *What exactly had made it work?*

She wrote two lists.

## What the setup did

| The part | What it did this week | Without it |
|---|---|---|
| **The project** | Gave every run the same rules, and kept Tuesday's new one | Someone pastes the rules each time, and forgets one |
| **The skill** | Wrote both memos in Sana's way, and named the returned check an unrecorded item | The memo depends on who wrote it |
| **The shared drive,** connected | Gave every run, and the morning bank check, the same files | The files wait on one person's laptop |
| **The folder,** through the desktop app | Let Claude read Dana's three files where they were. Held the bank's statement for Hamza's tests. | Hamza uploads files one by one |
| **The borrowed desk** | Matched 3,400 lines in minutes, the same way each time: in three runs, two on Tuesday and one on Wednesday | Eight hours, and tired eyes |
| **The artifacts** | Held the pack as four pieces that every run updated | New files each time, with old ones left around |
| **The morning bank check** (the worker's routine) | Looked at the bank when nobody had asked | The firm finds out from the client |

No part was new. Each one is taught in an earlier section of this book ([Exhibit 1](exhibits.md#exhibit-1-where-the-earlier-sections-appear-in-this-case)). What was new was the joining.

## What people did

![What the setup did, and what people did. Two columns. On the left, the setup: read the files, matched 3,400 lines, worked out the figure, built four pieces, ran again on Wednesday, watched the bank each morning. On the right, people: wrote the rules and the method, checked the result without Claude, approved by name and figure, stopped a fix by hand, told the client. Nisha looked at the two lists side by side.

*"The setup did the work. We did the judgment. Before this week we spent most of our hours on the first list."*

## Is this better than a document tool?

Hamza asked the question a student would ask. *"Dana's old accountant used Google Docs and Slides. Those have AI in them now too. What did we really gain?"*

Nisha answered in two parts.

*"For writing, nothing. Google's editors are very good. They also keep a better record of earlier versions of slides than ours do. If the job were to write a memo, I would not care which one we used."*[^google-history],[^deck-history] Claude's sheets have a version history, and its Docs page speaks of one.[^sheet-history],[^docs-history]

*"But the job was never the memo. The job was the bank lines, the matching, the rule about returned checks, the four pieces that must agree, and the morning bank check. A document editor starts when someone has the figure. Our setup starts with the bank's file and ends with the pack."*

She added that the comparison has limits. The firm had not tested Google's AI features. And another firm could join other tools into a setup too.

![A document editor and a workplace. Six steps of one month-end pack in a row: read the bank's file, match 3,400 lines, work out the figure, apply the method, write the pieces, do it again next month. Under them, two rows. A document editor, with AI help: a person does steps 1 to 4 and step 6, and AI helps with step 5, writing the pieces; it helps when someone already has the figure. The firm's setup: Claude does all six steps; it starts at the bank's file and runs again next month. A line says that in both, a person checks, approves and tells the client. A band at the foot says the slow part of this job was never the writing.](img/a-document-editor-and-a-workplace.png)

In both, people are responsible. She underlined that.

## Where it can still go wrong

Sana did not work Fridays. On Thursday evening she had left a list on Nisha's desk: what the setup does not do ([Exhibit 12](exhibits.md#exhibit-12-before-you-trust-a-setup)).

- **Instructions only ask.** The project says *"never send anything"*, but what stops a send is a setting: only the shared drive is connected to Hamza's account.[^claude-projects] And that setting is Hamza's. His project is outside the firm's Team plan, so the settings Nisha controls as its Owner do not reach it.
- **The worker is different.** Its Slack channel still holds `reports@`, so the worker could send. It also runs the morning bank check. For the worker, *"change nothing in the pack"* is only a sentence. What protects the firm there is its habit: a named approval before anything leaves.
- **The pieces are still copies.** No piece reads another.[^no-source] One run writes them all, and a person checks that they agree.
- **Hamza's tests missed one thing.** They never compared the run note's balance with the bank's statement.
- **A program can match wrongly.** Two payments of the same amount on the same day can be paired the wrong way round. Hamza's twenty pairs are a sample, not a proof.
- **The morning bank check only looks where it is told to.** It saw the returned check because it downloaded the bank's lines again. It would not see a mistake in Northgate's ledger.
- **The first download was too early.** Tuesday's run used Friday's file, which was four days old. But the age of a file is only a warning. A file can be an hour old and still be too early. The bank's statement is the test.
- **One person can now do a lot, fast.** That is the point of the setup. It is also why the approval must stay with a named person.

At the bottom Sana had written: *"It did two days of work in twenty-five minutes. It can do two days of damage in twenty-five minutes too. I will keep reading everything I sign."*

## The firm's new rule and new habit

On Friday morning Nisha wrote a rule, and Hamza added it to the instructions, after the line about the newest bank file: *"Before you start, compare its closing balance with the bank's statement for the month, in the same folder. If the two differ, or there is no statement yet, stop and say so. If the file was downloaded more than a day ago, say that too. In the run note, name the ledger file you used and its date."* Hamza's first test now makes the same comparison. Before each run, he asks the worker in Slack for a fresh download, and saves a copy of the bank's statement to the month's folder in the drive. He keeps his own copy for his tests.

## What the firm had now

By Friday the firm owned something it had not owned on Monday: a setup that could do Northgate's month-end again in October, and in November. Most of it would work for another client with one new project.

Nisha saw that this was worth more than the pack. She also saw the question that Northgate's owner was going to ask.
