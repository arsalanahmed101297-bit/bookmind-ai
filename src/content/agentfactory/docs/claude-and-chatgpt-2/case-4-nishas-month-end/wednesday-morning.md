## 9:00 a.m., Wednesday

> **New words on this page.** **Returned check:** a check that the bank of the person who wrote it refused to pay, so the money is taken back.


On Wednesday, October 6, the morning bank check ran for the first time. At 9:00 a.m. in Karachi, the worker downloaded September's bank lines again. It compared the bank's closing balance with Tuesday's run note. Then it posted in the firm's channel.

> *"The bank's lines for September have changed since Friday's download. There is one new line, dated September 30: a returned check from Erie Street Diner, $3,450.00. Northgate deposited that check on September 28. The bank's closing balance is now $198,030.00, not $201,480.00. Reconciled cash would be $194,850.00. That is $150.00 below the bank's minimum of $195,000.00. I have saved the new download to the drive. I have changed nothing in the pack."*

The diner's check had failed, so Northgate's bank took the money back. Why had Friday's file not shown it? The worker downloaded that file early on October 1, Cleveland time. The bank had not yet added all of September 30's lines.

In Cleveland it was midnight. Dana was asleep, with Tuesday's pack in her inbox and a meeting with her bank on Thursday at 10 a.m.

## The old habit

Hamza did the sum on paper. $198,030.00 plus $6,120.00 minus $9,300.00 is $194,850.00. The worker was right.

Then he opened the bank's statement in his own folder, the one he had downloaded on Monday. It already showed the returned check. On Tuesday his tests had used it to read twenty pairs, but his arithmetic had used the closing balance from Claude's run note. If he had compared that balance with his statement, he would have found the error a day earlier.

Then he did what he had always done. He opened the deck and began to change slide 2.

Sana was at the next desk. *"Stop. How many places hold that figure?"*

He counted. Besides slide 2, five more places were now wrong. Three showed the figure. Two showed words that depend on it, like READY and *above*. The cover has no figure. Here are the five:

- the sheet;
- the memo's figure;
- the memo's first line, which still said READY;
- the memo's comparison with the bank's minimum, which still said *above*;
- slide 3, which shows the figure and the list of items.

*"You will fix slide 2 and forget one of the others,"* she said. *"I have done it myself, for fifteen years. The setup made the pack. Let the setup make it again."*

## The second run

At 9:40 a.m. Hamza typed a new request in the Northgate project.

> *"The bank's lines for September have changed. Prepare the September 2027 month-end pack again, from the newest download in the drive."*

Claude read the new file, matched the lines again, and found one bank line with no ledger line: the returned check. Northgate's books still counted the diner's $3,450.00 as cash, but the bank had taken it back. The books would be wrong until Dana recorded it. Sana's skill had a rule for this. Something the bank shows and the books do not is an **unrecorded item**, and the status is NOT READY.

By 10:00 a.m. the four pieces were updated, and the run note said what had changed since Tuesday.

| | Tuesday's run | Wednesday's run |
|---|---|---|
| Bank's closing balance | $201,480.00 | $198,030.00 |
| Add: deposits the bank has not shown yet | $6,120.00 | $6,120.00 |
| Subtract: checks the bank has not paid yet | $9,300.00 | $9,300.00 |
| **Reconciled cash** | **$198,300.00** | **$194,850.00** |
| Against the bank's minimum | $3,300.00 above | $150.00 below |
| Status | READY | NOT READY, 1 unrecorded item |

Hamza did his four tests again. This time they took forty minutes, because he knew where to look. The sheet, the memo and slide 2 all said $194,850.00. He saved the new PDFs to the month's folder in the drive.

## A new approval

The new pack was a new piece of work, so it needed its own approval. Nisha was with another client. Sana could also approve, and she works Monday to Thursday.

At 11:15 a.m. she wrote in the channel:

*"Approved to send: Northgate September month-end pack, second version. Status NOT READY, one unrecorded item: the returned check, $3,450.00. Reconciled cash $194,850.00. It goes to Dana so that she can record the check and tell her bank. It replaces Tuesday's pack."*

Her approval did not change the status. The books still did not agree with the bank, and only Dana could fix that. Sana did not approve the books. She approved sending this pack, with its unrecorded item named, because the client needed it before Thursday. A NOT READY pack leaves the firm only this way: a named person decides, and the approval says what is still open.

At 11:30 a.m. Sana sent the new PDFs to Dana from her own work address, with a short message she wrote herself.

> *"Dana, this replaces the pack we sent on Tuesday. The bank returned a check from Erie Street Diner, $3,450.00, and dated it September 30. Reconciled cash is $194,850.00, not $198,300.00. That is $150.00 below your bank's minimum. Your books do not show the returned check yet. Please use this pack on Thursday, not Tuesday's."*

In Cleveland it was 2:30 a.m.

## Wednesday and Thursday in Cleveland

Dana read the message at 8 a.m. on Wednesday, her time. She recorded the returned check in Northgate's books and called Tom at her bank that afternoon.

On Thursday at 10 a.m. she put one pack on the table. Tom already knew the figure.

*"A hundred and fifty dollars under, and you called me before I found it,"* he said. *"Send me a note about the diner's check. That is all I need."*
