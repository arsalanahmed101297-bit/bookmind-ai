With a borrowed desk, an organizer (the harness), connectors and scheduled tasks, Claude and ChatGPT can now act as **AI Workers**. You hand them a job, and they do it again and again, even while you are away.

For personal and team work, that is ready today. You can set up a weekly report, a daily inbox summary or a research digest yourself. For bigger personal work, a [project](projects-one-team-lead-many-desks.md) adds a coordinator that runs many jobs at once and carries your brief and lessons across them.

For work that touches **money, customers or important records**, a business must add four things around the AI. Think of hiring a new employee:

| What a business adds | Why | Like a new employee's |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Its own identity** | Actions must show they came from the AI Worker, not from whoever set it up. | Staff ID badge |
| **A lasting record** | Progress and decisions must be saved in a lasting record, such as a database, not only in the chat | Company files |
| **Enforced approvals** | Important actions must be checked by the systems that perform them | Controller's signature |
| **Monitoring** | Someone must see what the worker did, what it cost, and when it failed | Performance review |

In practice, businesses keep the AI as the engine and build these pieces around it, often with their own connector, a login system and a database. [The Architecture](../architecture/overview.md) explains these options.

Claude Tag and self-hosted environments start this ([Setting up the desk](setting-up-the-desk-keys-buildings-and-badges.md)). The approvals and the lasting record are still yours to add.
