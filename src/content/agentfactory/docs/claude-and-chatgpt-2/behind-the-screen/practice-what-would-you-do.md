Here are six short scenarios. Use the team picture from this section to decide, and write your answer before you read the answers below.

### The scenarios

1. **The lost file.** Yesterday the AI Worker made a chart on its borrowed desk, but it never gave the file to you. Today you ask for it, and it says the file is gone. What happened, and what will you do next time?
2. **The weekly report.** You want a scheduled task to compare this week's payment run with last week's. Where should the task find last week's results?
3. **The important email.** The AI Worker wants to send a message to all your customers. It asks you to approve it. Should you approve without reading? Why or why not?
4. **The company plan.** A company wants an AI Worker to prepare supplier payments every week. Name two things the company must add before it trusts the AI Worker near money.
5. **The project rule.** You run Brightline's payment run as a Claude project. You tell the coordinator: "Never start more than two threads, and never pay a supplier without my yes." Which part of that message is safe to rely on, and what else do you need?
6. **The team worker.** Brightline's finance team wants Claude in its Slack channel. Dave, the controller, asks two questions: "Will its work look like it came from me? And where should the accounting key go?" What would you answer?

### Answers

1. The desk was cleared after a quiet period, and the file was only kept there. Next time, ask for the file, or have it saved to Drive, as soon as it is made.
2. In a lasting place the task saved to last week, like a Drive file or a shared sheet. The new run does not remember last week's run.
3. No. Your yes is the second signature. Read the message first. The AI Worker can make mistakes or be tricked by hidden instructions.
4. Any two of these: its own identity, a lasting record of its work, permissions and approvals checked by the payment system, and monitoring.
5. Neither part is a lock. The coordinator saves both as preferences and follows them, but Anthropic says such rules are not enforced settings. For payments, the yes must be checked by the payment system itself. See [Projects](projects-one-team-lead-many-desks.md).
6. In the channel, Claude Tag works under its own service accounts, so its work usually shows Claude's badge, not Dave's name. The accounting key belongs at reception: an organization Owner or a Claude Tag admin sets it up for Claude Tag, and the proxy adds it, so it is never in the chat or on the desk. The payment still needs Dave's yes, checked by the payment system. One more thing: in a one-to-one message with Claude, it works as Dave, with Dave's own connectors.
