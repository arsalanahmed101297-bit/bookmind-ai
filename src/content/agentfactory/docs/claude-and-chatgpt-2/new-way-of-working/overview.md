*What the new AI Desktop Apps make possible*

## What has changed

In 2026 Anthropic and OpenAI, the companies behind Claude and ChatGPT, rebuilt their apps so that the AI inside them can do whole jobs. You describe the result you want. The app does the job step by step, on its own. It reads your files, uses a web browser and your other apps, and does its math with small programs, which is more reliable than doing it in its head. Some jobs keep running after you close your laptop. Then it hands back finished work, such as a document, a spreadsheet or a presentation, for you to check.[^apps],[^openai-ambitious-work],[^openai-use-chatgpt]

The AI itself, called the **model**, also improved. But that is not the main change, so the most useful skill is no longer asking good questions. It is handing over work well and checking it well.

## What should we call these apps?

This book calls them **AI Worker platforms**. You will often hear them called **agentic AI platforms**. *Agentic* means the AI can act on its own, step by step, toward a goal you set. A *platform* is the software that the work runs on. That name puts the focus on the AI. But the apps also give the AI a computer to work on, your files and apps to work with, settings that limit what it may do, and ways for you to follow and check its work. So this book uses a more exact name. An AI Worker platform is a workplace for AI Workers. You hand out the work there, and you check it.
