This is the detailed tour behind [The New Way of Working](../new-way-of-working/overview.md). It shows each feature, compares the two apps, and advises which model to use for which job.

## How should you use this tour?

You do not need to read it all now, or remember it. Skim it to see what the apps can do, and come back when a chapter uses a feature. Words like repository, worktree and pull request belong to software work. You can skip any paragraph that uses them.

## Which words and dates should you know?

> [!NOTE]
> **Words used in this tour**
>
> - **Model:** the AI itself, such as Claude Opus 5.5 or GPT-6.2 Astra. The app is the program around it.
> - **Task:** a job the AI works through on its own, step by step, instead of answering in one reply.
> - **Context:** everything the AI holds in mind at once while it works.
> - **Connector:** a link from the AI to another app, such as your email or a database. Most use an open standard called MCP.
> - **Skill:** saved instructions that teach the AI how to do one kind of job your way.
> - **Plugin:** a package of skills and connectors you install together.
> - **Permission setting:** how much the AI may do before it asks you.
> - **Effort:** how hard the model thinks before answering. More effort is slower and uses more of your plan.
> - **Sandbox:** a closed-off space or computer where the AI works, so it cannot reach anything outside.
> - **Repository:** a project folder whose history of changes is tracked, often stored online at GitHub.
> - **Pull request:** a proposed change to a project's files, which someone reviews before it is added.

> [!NOTE]
> **How to read the product detail in this tour**
>
> Product detail changes monthly, and features differ by plan and region. Each page carries its own "as verified" date. Facts come from the vendors' own documentation unless marked *(reported)*, which means they come from walkthroughs or news reports and were not found in the vendor's documentation. Rollout dates and plan details are in the footnotes. If your app looks different, the vendor's help page is the authority, and the ideas still hold.
