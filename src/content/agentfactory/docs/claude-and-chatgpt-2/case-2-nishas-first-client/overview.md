*How a company of one ran an IFRS to US GAAP job with an AI Worker, and what she would not let it do*

Panaversity case study · November 2026

## Where this case fits

This is the seventh section of Claude and ChatGPT 2.0, and its second case study. [Case 1](../case-1-nishas-ai-team/overview.md) showed Nisha managing two AI tools while she sat in front of them. It stayed at the first step of [the ladder from helper to AI Worker](../behind-the-screen/from-helper-to-ai-worker.md): a helper used by a person who is present. This case climbs higher. Nisha now owns an AI Worker that runs while she is away, inside limits she sets.

- **The brief.** [One job, start to finish](../new-way-of-working/one-job.md) showed the four parts of a brief. Here, the brief becomes the project's standing instructions.
- **Work while you sleep.** [Work that runs while you sleep](../behind-the-screen/work-that-runs-while-you-sleep.md) showed that a scheduled run cannot count on remembering your conversation. Watch what Nisha has to write down so that a run at 1 a.m. can do the job alone.
- **A project with threads.** [Projects: one coordinator, many desks](../behind-the-screen/projects-one-team-lead-many-desks.md) showed one conversation handing jobs to threads. Nisha uses one thread for each part of the job, and one to review.
- **Saving work somewhere lasting.** [Why the borrowed desk gets cleared](../behind-the-screen/why-the-borrowed-desk-gets-cleared.md) showed that the desk can be cleared. Every version of Nisha's work is saved outside it.
- **The second signature.** [Staying safe](../behind-the-screen/staying-safe-a-locked-office-and-a-second-signature.md) showed that a person makes the final decision. Here, the question is who sends anything to the client.
- **Identity and the business layer.** [From personal worker to business worker](../architecture/from-personal-worker-to-business-worker.md) asked whose name a worker acts under. On Thursday, that question stops being theory.

> **About this case**
>
> This is a fictional teaching case, prepared for class discussion.
>
> **Made up for teaching:** Nisha, the buyer, the exporter, every figure in the accounts, the fee, the deadline, the emails and Nisha's thoughts. No real company or client data is used.
>
> **What was run:** Case 1 was based on a practical exercise that was carried out and recorded. Case 2's practical was run on November 17, 2026, in one sitting. Each nightly run was started by hand instead of at 1 a.m., no usage limit was reached, and no person did Nisha's part. The run log, what the worker and ChatGPT caught, and the token cost are recorded. Nisha's own time, her own list of problems and the limit stop on the second night are not recorded. They follow the story and Anthropic's docs. One run cannot show what an AI Worker always does, so read what the worker "caught" or "missed" as what might happen, not as findings. The [practical run sheet and record](../../appendices/instructors/case-2/appendix-practical-run-sheet.md#the-record) has the details.[^run-sheet]
>
> **Not accounting advice.** The accounting differences are real and well known, but the bridge in this case is a short teaching example of selected items. It is not a full conversion and not audit-ready. It needs a qualified accountant's check before anyone relies on it.
>
> The case does not say whether Nisha's choices were right or wrong.
