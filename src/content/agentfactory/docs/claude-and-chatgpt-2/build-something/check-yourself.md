Check what you remember from these pages. Answer each flashcard in your head, then turn it over and choose Got it or Missed it. Cards you do not know yet come back sooner. The quiz mixes the questions from these pages and asks 10 at a time.

## Are you ready for the cases?

**The pass mark.** You are ready when you have done [One job, three deliverables](one-job-three-deliverables.md) without looking at the answers, and every line of its **You are done when** list is true. If a line is not true, fix it and check again before you start the cases.

The cases ask you to do four things. The exercise practices each one, and these questions show what to practice again:

1. **Choose the right tool.** For a job, can you say whether you need a doc, slides, a sheet, a design, a data dashboard or a whiteboard? Can you say when you need a file or a website instead, and say in your request where the result should go? In the exercise: the sheet, the doc and the slides each did a different job.
2. **Check what the AI made.** Can you check a total yourself? Can you point each claim to its source, and find a claim that has none? In the exercise: checks 1 to 3.
3. **Control what is shared and what is done.** Before you share, can you say who will see what? Can you name the steps that only a person may take, such as approving a payment? In the exercise: step 4 and checks 4 and 5.
4. **Combine the parts.** Can you say when to add a project, a folder on your computer or a scheduled task to your work? Can you say why a sheet built from a file does not change when the file changes? In the exercise: the optional **Going further** part. It is not part of the pass mark.
