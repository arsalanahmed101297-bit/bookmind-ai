Every few years, the way people work with machines changes. What people hand to a machine has moved from a query, to a message, to a task. Now it is a role. By October 2026, the two leading AI vendors both offered an AI that keeps working on its own while you are away, and open-source tools let people run one on their own computers.[^openai-dots],[^claude-duet],[^openclaw] An AI Worker is an AI that holds that role. You assign, govern and manage it, much like anyone who holds a role on your team.

This book is about that change, and about where it leads. The five horizons below are the steps in that change, from the AI Agent era to a possible autonomous economy. They show which parts of the book prepare you for each step. The first edition covered Horizon 0, the AI Agent era, and this book keeps its principles. You start at Horizon 1, the AI Worker era, which has just begun. If you have done the first section, [Claude and ChatGPT 2.0](claude-and-chatgpt-2/overview.md), you have already taken the first step at this horizon. With Nisha, you handed an AI whole jobs, set its limits and checked its work.

## The five horizons

This is the book's outlook as of October 2026. Read the figure from the bottom up. The dates are indicative, which means they are not exact. The future horizons are forecasts, and Horizon 4 is only a possible future. The horizons also overlap. Parts IV to VIII are not written yet. Parts V to VII will prepare you for Horizons 2 and 3 before they arrive. Early signs of Horizon 2 are already visible. One vendor is testing ready-made AI Workers for companies, each with its own identity and system access for one role.[^openai-dots]

Chatbots are prompted, agents are delegated tasks, AI Workers are managed, and AI Workforces are composed. [Chapter 1](ai-worker-paradigm/from-chatbots-to-ai-workers/ladder-of-interaction.md) teaches this ladder.

## Where this book starts

Managing one AI Worker well is the foundation for coordinating a workforce. A country needs people and companies with these skills before it can produce and export AI work. So this book starts with one AI Worker, and how to manage it well.

[The Preface](preface.md) comes next. It shows this change in one person's work, and it defines the main terms used here.
