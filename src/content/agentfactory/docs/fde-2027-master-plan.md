**Train 10,000 Forward Deployed Engineers and build the next generation of AI startup founders.**[^panaversity-fde-2027]

The AI revolution is transforming software development, freelancing, entrepreneurship and the global job market. AI is increasingly capable of writing code, building applications, analyzing data and performing complex tasks.

The future of software engineering is not simply about writing code. It is increasingly about:

- understanding real business problems
- designing AI-powered solutions
- building and managing AI Workers
- integrating AI into business systems
- evaluating reliability and performance
- delivering measurable business outcomes

We must prepare the workforce and entrepreneurs for the emerging AI Worker Economy.

## The rise of Forward Deployed Engineers

A **Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)** works directly with organizations to understand business problems, and builds, deploys and manages AI-powered solutions. Palantir pioneered the FDE model, and it is spreading across the technology industry.[^panaversity-fde-2027]

Major companies hiring FDEs, or investing in related AI deployment engineering, include:[^panaversity-fde-2027]

| Kind of company | Companies |
| --- | --- |
| AI labs | OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Cohere, Mistral AI |
| Cloud and enterprise | Microsoft, AWS, Salesforce, Databricks |
| AI-native companies | Scale AI, Glean, Distyl AI, Snorkel AI |
| Consulting and services | Accenture, Deloitte, Capgemini, Wipro |

These companies take different approaches to bringing AI engineering directly into their customers' environments. AWS has announced a $1 billion investment in a dedicated Forward Deployed Engineering organization: read [AWS's announcement](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/aws/aws-1-billion-forward-deployed-ai-engineers).[^aws-fde]

The opportunity is clear: companies increasingly need engineers who can solve real business problems with AI, not just write software.

## The AI startup opportunity

The AI revolution will not only create new jobs. It will make entirely new companies possible.

In the past, building a software startup often needed a large development team, a lot of capital and months of engineering work. AI is changing that. Small teams, and sometimes a single founder, can now use AI coding agents and AI Workers to build products, automate operations and serve customers. Thousands of AI-native startups are likely to emerge worldwide, and some may become unicorns: companies valued at $1 billion or more.[^panaversity-fde-2027]

The skills that make a successful FDE can also make an AI startup founder:

- identify valuable business problems
- build AI products with small teams
- develop AI Workers and automation
- connect products to real business systems
- launch faster and test with customers
- scale products for global markets

An FDE solves business problems with AI. An AI startup founder turns those solutions into scalable businesses. We want our learners to create AI companies, not just work for them.

## The FDE 2027 mission

The mission is to train 10,000 Forward Deployed Engineers and empower the next generation of AI startup founders.[^panaversity-fde-2027] It prepares three kinds of people:

- **Forward Deployed Engineers**, who deliver production-ready AI solutions.
- **AI startup founders**, who build products, launch companies and create jobs.
- **AI business leaders**, who transform organizations with AI Workers.

The objective is not only to teach AI tools or to issue certificates. It is to build the ability to solve problems, create value and build globally competitive businesses.

## Why now?

AI capabilities are advancing quickly, and they make more and more capable autonomous AI systems possible.

A milestone came on 19 November 2026, when Anthropic, the company behind Claude, began trading on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT. Its IPO raised about $81.3 billion, at a fully diluted offering valuation of about $1.5 trillion.[^panaversity-fde-2027] It shows how much investment is flowing into AI.

Panaversity prepares its students to work with Anthropic, OpenAI and the wider AI ecosystem. Two Anthropic resources guide this journey:

1. [Claude Certifications, version 1.1](https://anthropic-partners.skilljar.com/page/partner-certifications)[^anthropic-partner-certifications]
2. [The Claude Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) learning path](https://anthropic-partners.skilljar.com/path/claude-fde)[^anthropic-claude-fde-path]

## The four-stage FDE certification pathway

This book, [*The AI Agent Factory*, second edition](https://agentfactory.panaversity.org/), is the main study resource.[^panaversity-fde-2027] [Appendix C](appendices/exams/associate-foundations/overview.md) maps every skill in the CCAO-F exam guide, version 1.1, to the pages that teach it. Anthropic's CCDV-F guide, version 1.1, is linked under Exam 4.

The training has four exams. Each one covers more of the book than the one before it:

| Exam | Name | What it prepares you for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | PCAO-F Basic | AI basic concepts and usage |
| 2 | PCAO-F Full | Claude Certified Associate: Foundations (CCAO-F) |
| 3 | PCDV-F Connectors | Building MCP servers and interactive MCP apps with AI coding agents |
| 4 | PCDV-F Full | Claude Certified Developer: Foundations (CCDV-F) |

Exams 1 and 2 do not depend on each other: Exam 1 is a step that helps you prepare in stages, and you can take Exam 2 directly ([Appendix C](appendices/exams/associate-foundations/overview.md#the-pcao-f-exams)). Exams 3 and 4 come after PCAO-F, in that order. Faculty, mentors and startup founders are strongly encouraged to follow the same pathway.[^panaversity-fde-2027]

> [!IMPORTANT]
> PCAO-F and PCDV-F are Panaversity's own benchmark exams. They prepare you for the matching official Anthropic certifications, but they do not give you those certifications.

**Exam format.** Every computer exam uses scenario-based multiple-choice questions. They test practical understanding, reasoning and application, not memorization. Exams 1 and 3 are computer exams only. Exams 2 and 4 are a computer exam, then a written exam for candidates who pass the computer exam.[^panaversity-fde-2027]

### Exam 1: PCAO-F Basic (the Basic Concepts Exam)

AI basic concepts and usage.

- **Who can take it.** Anyone who enters the FDE 2027 pathway. It is optional.
- **What it tests.** [Claude and ChatGPT 2.0](claude-and-chatgpt-2/overview.md), [The Five Horizons](five-horizons.md), the [Preface](preface.md) and [Part 0](how-ai-works/overview.md).
- **Computer exam.** 35 scenario-based multiple-choice questions in 60 minutes.
- **Written exam.** None.
- **What comes next.** Take Exam 2 when you have mastered its complete syllabus.
- **Practice.** The [Basic Concepts sample exam](appendices/exams/associate-foundations/basic-sample-exam.md).

### Exam 2: PCAO-F Full (the Full Certification Exam)

Preparation for Claude Certified Associate: Foundations (CCAO-F).

- **Who can take it.** Anyone. You do not need to pass Exam 1 first.
- **What it tests.** The book from its beginning to the end of Part II: everything in Exam 1, and also [Part I](ai-worker-paradigm/overview.md) and [Part II](managing-ai-workers/overview.md).
- **Computer exam.** 70 scenario-based multiple-choice questions in 120 minutes.
- **Written exam.** 15 questions in 30 minutes, taken straight after you pass the computer exam.
- **What comes next.** Passing both parts qualifies you for Exam 3.
- **Practice.** The [PCAO-F sample exam](appendices/exams/associate-foundations/sample-exam.md).
- **Preparation guide.** [Appendix C. Preparing for PCAO-F and CCAO-F](appendices/exams/associate-foundations/overview.md).
- **Anthropic's guide.** The official [CCAO-F exam guide, version 1.1](https://cc.sj-cdn.net/instructor/6nizmqk8tpzpfjvt6qmmav7rh/public/1794946609/Claude_Certified_Associate_-_Foundations_Exam_Guide_Updated_Dec_7.pdf).[^anthropic-ccao-f-guide]

### Exam 3: PCDV-F Connectors

Building interactive connectors with AI coding agents. This stage is about building software, connecting business applications, and extending AI Workers with MCP.

- **Who can take it.** Students who passed Exam 2 and hold PCAO-F.
- **What it tests.** Part III, Chapters 12 to 20:
  - [Chapter 12. Agentic Coding: Claude Code, Codex and OpenCode](building-ai-workers/agentic-coding/overview.md)
  - [Chapter 13. Programming Fundamentals in the AI Era: TypeScript and Python](building-ai-workers/programming-fundamentals/overview.md)
  - [Chapter 14. Structuring Programs in the AI Era: Objects, Data and Async](building-ai-workers/structuring-programs/overview.md)
  - [Chapter 15. Trusting Code in the AI Era: Tests, Types, Pull Requests and Code Review](building-ai-workers/code-review/overview.md)
  - [Chapter 16. Building Skills and Plugins with Coding Agents](building-ai-workers/skills-and-plugins/overview.md)
  - [Chapter 17. Building Connectors: Tool Design and MCP Servers](building-ai-workers/connectors-and-mcp/overview.md)
  - [Chapter 18. Securing Connectors: OAuth for MCP, with Better Auth and D1](building-ai-workers/securing-connectors/overview.md)
  - [Chapter 19. Business Data and Retrieval: Postgres, Row-Level Security and pgvector, on Neon](building-ai-workers/business-data-and-retrieval/overview.md)
  - [Chapter 20. Building Interactive Connectors: MCP Apps and Extensions](building-ai-workers/mcp-apps/overview.md)
- **Computer exam.** 70 scenario-based multiple-choice questions in 120 minutes.
- **Written exam.** None.
- **What comes next.** Passing Exam 3 qualifies you for Exam 4.

### Exam 4: PCDV-F Full

Preparation for Claude Certified Developer: Foundations (CCDV-F). This stage builds the skills to design, build, evaluate, secure and deploy production-ready AI Workers.

- **Who can take it.** Students who passed Exams 2 and 3.
- **What it tests.** Part III, Chapters 12 to 30: everything in Exam 3, and also these chapters.
  - AI agents and architecture:
    - [Chapter 21. The Agent Loop by Hand: Claude Messages and OpenAI Responses](building-ai-workers/agent-loop-by-hand/overview.md)
    - [Chapter 22. Agent SDKs: the Claude Agent SDK and the OpenAI Agents SDK](building-ai-workers/agent-sdks/overview.md)
    - [Chapter 23. Hosted Agents: Claude, OpenAI and Open Source](building-ai-workers/hosted-agents/overview.md)
    - Chapter 25. Prompts and Structured Output for Applications
    - Chapter 26. Context Engineering for Long-Running Agents
    - Chapter 27. Agent Architecture: Workflows, Agents and Subagents
  - Production engineering:
    - Chapter 24. Evaluating AI Workers: Golden Sets, Judges and Failure Modes
    - Chapter 28. Guardrails and Agent Security
    - Chapter 29. Model Selection, Cost and Release Planning
    - Chapter 30. Shipping Claude Applications: Files, Surfaces and Resilience
- **Computer exam.** 70 scenario-based multiple-choice questions in 120 minutes.
- **Written exam.** 15 questions in 30 minutes, taken straight after you pass the computer exam.
- **Anthropic's guide.** The official [CCDV-F exam guide, version 1.1](https://cc.sj-cdn.net/instructor/6nizmqk8tpzpfjvt6qmmav7rh/public/1794946771/Claude_Certified_Developer_-_Foundations_Exam_Guide_Updated_Dec_7.pdf).[^anthropic-ccdv-f-guide]

Some of these advanced chapters are still being written, and they will be released one by one. Begin with the chapters that are published (the linked ones above), and watch for later syllabus announcements.[^panaversity-fde-2027]

## The vision

The four-stage pathway is the technical foundation. Real projects, understanding customers, engineering experience and practical delivery turn that knowledge into professional capability.[^panaversity-fde-2027] There are two big opportunities:

1. **Global AI engineering careers.** FDEs who deliver AI solutions for organizations at home and around the world.
2. **Globally competitive AI startups.** Founders who turn real business problems into AI-powered products, services and new companies.

The ambition is to help thousands of aspiring entrepreneurs explore AI business opportunities, in the hope that some will build globally successful companies.

## To students, faculty and startup founders

Do not prepare only to pass an exam or get a job. Prepare to solve real problems and create value.

- Study this book, *The AI Agent Factory*.
- Follow the four-stage certification pathway.
- Review the official Claude certification guides.
- Practice scenario-based questions.
- Build real applications and AI Workers.
- Develop production-ready engineering skills.
- Identify business problems worth solving.
- Explore launching an AI-native startup.

**FDE 2027: 10,000 Forward Deployed Engineers, thousands of aspiring AI entrepreneurs, and a new generation of globally competitive AI startups.**

Learn the concepts. Prove your skills. Build real solutions. Create new companies. Do not simply consume the AI revolution: help build its future.
